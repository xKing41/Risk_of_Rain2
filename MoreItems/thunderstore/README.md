# MoreItems

Adds **102 new items** to Risk of Rain 2:

| Rarity | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Common | 39 |
| Uncommon | 35 |
| Legendary | 14 |
| Boss | 6 |
| Lunar | 8 |

Mechanics: stat boosts, on-hit effects (burn, bleed, lightning, missiles, stun, slow), on-kill effects
(explosions, healing, gold, buffs), on-hurt effects (thorns, barrier, counter-lightning), conditional bonuses
(standing still, sprinting, airborne, low or full health, in or out of combat) and periodic effects
(lightning aura, healing totem, missiles, supernova).

Every item can be turned off in `BepInEx/config/xking41.MoreItems.cfg`.
